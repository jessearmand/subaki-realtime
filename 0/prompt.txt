   - PCM16 audio over the WS (no codec): mic → Float32 → PCM16 base64 → `input_audio_buffer.append`; inbound `response.output_audio.delta` → PCM16 → AudioBuffer playback. Native AudioContext sample rate advertised in `session.update` so no resampling.
